# Medium Priority Ideas

These ideas are useful and worth keeping, but they should come after the core portfolio experience is in good shape.

## Content and navigation
- **Series support for blog posts** — multi-part articles with next/prev navigation
- **Archives page** — group posts by month or year
- **Bookmarks collection** — curated links and resources
- **Changelog page** — visible history of portfolio updates
- **Revision history** — show update dates and recent changes more clearly

## Light integration ideas
- **GitHub activity widget** — recent commits or PRs on the homepage or about page
- **Progressive image loading** — nicer image placeholders for large visual content
