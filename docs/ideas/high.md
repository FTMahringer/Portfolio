# High Priority Ideas

These ideas have the strongest payoff for a portfolio/blog site.

## Core site value
- **Testimonials system** — social proof from colleagues, clients, or collaborators
- **Portfolio stats** — total projects, posts, and years of experience
- **Timeline view** — combine projects and experience into a clear career timeline
- **Related posts** — better content discovery at the end of blog posts
- **Education section** — a structured source for resume and profile data

## Important future feature
- **Automated PDF resume generation** — generate a downloadable resume from existing content sources
  - Use experience entries, education, skills, and bio data
  - Keep the layout maintainable and reusable
